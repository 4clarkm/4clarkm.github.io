Projects

Statistical Arbitrage - V/MA Pairs Trading Strategy

A market neutral pairs trading strategy exploiting the cointegrated relationship between Visa and Mastercard. Built a full backtesting framework with rolling hedge ratios, cointegration testing, transaction costs, Monte Carlo simulation, and stress testing. The strategy returned +10.9% during the COVID crash and +5.3% during the 2022 rate shock while the broader market fell - a direct demonstration of market neutrality in practice. Deployed as a live interactive Streamlit dashboard.

Momentum Regime Analysis - S&P 500 Sector ETFs

A cross-sectional momentum strategy across 11 S&P 500 sector ETFs. Constructed momentum signals, tested regime-switching rules conditioned on bull and bear market environments, and implemented inverse volatility weighting. Found that dropping the short leg in bull markets improved the Sharpe ratio from -0.27 to 0.40 and cut max drawdown from -76% to -31%.
